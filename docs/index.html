Technology

Thermweave’s finishes add new properties to fabrics customers already use. They keep the fabric and suppliers they know.

Talk to us
New properties on familiar fabric

Our finishes give a fabric abilities it did not have, starting with thermal management and electrical conductivity. The fabric underneath, and the way it feels, does not change.

Applied on our own line

Finishes are applied on a line built for them. Customers send the fabric they already use and get it back finished.

Each finish is made for the job

Different finishes do different things, and each is set for the fabric and the use. Thermal properties, for example, can be tuned to move heat through a fabric or spread it across it.

Roll to roll, we change what a fabric can do.

Thermweave T100

Thermal conductivity
for ordinary fabrics.

T100 is Thermweave’s thermally conductive finish. It can pull heat away from the body or spread warmth from a heat source across a garment, without changing the fabric’s weight or feel.

Accessibility

Accessibility statement

Last updated 4 September 2026

Our commitment

Thermweave Inc. aims to make this website usable by as many people as possible, including people who rely on assistive technology. The site is built to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA where practical.

What that means here

  • Images that carry information have text alternatives. Decorative animations are hidden from screen readers.
  • Motion follows your system setting. With reduced motion turned on, animated sections show a single still frame.
  • Every link and button can be reached and used with a keyboard.
  • Headings are in a logical order, and the page reads correctly without styles.

Ongoing work

Accessibility is not finished when a page goes live. We review the site as it changes and fix problems as we find them.

Tell us if something doesn’t work

If you have trouble using any part of this site, or need information in another format, email isaac@thermweave.com. Tell us what you were trying to do and where it went wrong, and we’ll get back to you.

Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated 4 September 2026

This website belongs to Thermweave Inc. It is a plain informational site. It has no accounts, no forms and no advertising, and it does not set cookies or run analytics. What little personal information reaches us comes from you choosing to send it.

What we collect

If you email us, we receive what you send, usually your name, your email address, where you work and your message. We use it to reply and to follow up on what you asked about, and for nothing else.

The company that hosts this site may keep standard server logs, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you requested, to run and secure the service. We do not combine those logs with anything else or use them to identify you.

The site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser requests the font files from Google, so Google receives your IP address for that request. Google’s privacy policy covers how it handles that.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with anyone outside Thermweave except the providers that host the site and our email, or where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep correspondence for as long as we need it to deal with your request and any follow-up. You can ask us at any time to see what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, by emailing the address below.

Which law applies

Thermweave Inc. is a private company in New Brunswick, Canada. Our handling of personal information follows the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). If you are outside Canada and have rights under your own privacy law, you can exercise them through the same address.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site changes. Changes take effect when they are published here, and the date at the top will move with them.
